🌾 Paddy Farmers

🐛 Pesticide Management

Learn about common pests, symptoms and safe pest management practices for paddy crops.

🐛 1. Common Pests

Learn about common pests that can affect paddy crops and understand their visible signs.

🔍 2. Visible Symptoms

Observe the crop regularly for visible symptoms caused by pests.

🌱 3. Integrated Pest Management

Use suitable pest management practices and monitor the crop before taking action.

🏷️ 4. Label-Based Use

Always read and follow the instructions given on the product label.

⚠️ 5. Safe Handling & Storage

  • Handle products carefully.
  • Follow the safety instructions on the label.
  • Store products safely.
  • Keep them away from children and animals.

🚫 6. Avoid Unnecessary Application

Do not use pesticides unnecessarily. First observe the crop and identify the possible pest problem.
