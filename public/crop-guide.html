🌾 Paddy Crop Guide

1. Land Preparation

Prepare the field properly before planting paddy.

2. Seed Selection

Use good quality and healthy paddy seeds.

3. Nursery Preparation

Prepare a healthy nursery for growing paddy seedlings.

4. Transplanting

Transplant healthy seedlings into the prepared field.

5. Fertilizer Management

Apply fertilizers according to crop requirements.

6. Water Management

Maintain proper water levels throughout crop growth.

7. Pest and Disease Management

Regularly check the crop for pests and diseases.

8. Harvesting

Harvest the crop when the grains are properly matured.
