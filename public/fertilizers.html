🌾 Paddy Farmers

🧪 Fertilizer Management

Proper fertilizer management helps farmers understand the nutrient requirements of the paddy crop.

🌱 1. Basic Nutrient Requirements

Paddy crops require essential nutrients for healthy growth and development.

🧪 2. NPK Concept

NPK represents Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium. These are important nutrients for crop growth.

🌿 3. Organic Manure

Organic manure can be used as a source of nutrients and to support soil health.

📅 4. Application Stages

Fertilizer application should be planned according to the different stages of crop growth.

🔬 5. Soil Testing Importance

Soil testing helps understand the nutrient condition of the soil before fertilizer application.

⚠️ 6. Safety Precautions

  • Follow the recommended instructions.
  • Read product labels carefully.
  • Store fertilizers safely.
  • Keep fertilizers away from children and animals.
